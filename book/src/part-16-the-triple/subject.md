# Subject

The **Subject** is the identity that "possesses" or "initiates" the relation.

$$
\boxed{\textbf{The Subject is the starting point of the relation.}}
$$

## 16.3 Identity as Subject

A subject is an identity admitted to the system. It could represent:
- A Human (e.g., `User 123`)
- A Machine (e.g., `Sensor 456`)
- A Software Component (e.g., `Kernel 789`)

## 16.4 The Role of the Subject

The subject is always in the "Left" position of the triple. When we query the graph for "What does Subject X have?", we are looking for all triples where X occupies the subject position.
