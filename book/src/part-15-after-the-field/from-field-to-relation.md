# From Field to Relation

A field describes the internal condition of a subject. A relation describes the external connection between two subjects.

The transition from field to relation is the transition from **State** to **Fact**.

$$
\boxed{\textbf{A field is what a thing IS. A relation is what a thing DOES to another.}}
$$

## 15.3 The Subjectivity of the Field

When we say `HOLDER_ACTIVE`, we are describing a property admitted to the holder. This is an internal state. The field is subjective to the carrier.

## 15.4 The Objectivity of the Relation

When we say "Holder 123 *owns* Badge 456", we are describing a relation. This fact involves two identities and a predicate. This is a Triple.

- **Subject**: Holder 123
- **Predicate**: owns
- **Object**: Badge 456

This relation cannot be carried in an 8-bit field without losing the identities of the participants. To preserve the identities, we must move to a triple representation.

## 15.5 The Semantic Bridge

The field and the relation are not enemies. The relation often depends on the field. 
"Grant access *if* Holder is Active."
The `Active` state is a field. The `Grant` is a relation between Holder and Door.
