# Condition as Selected Outcome

A **Condition** is fundamentally different from a **Status**. 

Status is the raw evidence. It is what *is*. Status can be complex, multifaceted, and conflicting. A single entity might be `OK` and `STALE` and `WARN` all at once.

Condition is the *selected outcome*. It is what *must be done*. Condition cannot be multifaceted. Condition is the result of forcing multiplexed Statuses through a Selection Rule. The Selection Rule prioritizes, collapses, and simplifies the complex Status into exactly one Condition.

## The Funnel of Determinism

1. **Many Signals** enter the field.
2. **Multiple Statuses** are activated based on those signals.
3. A **Selection Rule** is applied to the active Statuses.
4. Exactly **One Condition** is selected.

This selected Condition is the only thing that drives subsequent system behavior. The complex raw Statuses are left behind in the receipt, while the selected Condition is pushed forward into the `Operation64` phase.
