# The Ninth Meaning

What happens when a domain requires a ninth meaning?
The naive approach is to widen the byte to a 16-bit word. The rigorous approach is to invoke the Need9 Law.

The Need9 Law states that when a ninth meaning is required, the field has suffered a category failure. You must not widen the field; instead, you must split the field, move to a relation, or establish a new independent record. The ninth meaning is evidence of missing structural depth.
