# Two Hundred Fifty-Six Activation States

Because the Semantic Byte is an independent multiplexer of eight boolean values, it has \(2^8 = 256\) possible activation states.
However, not all 256 states are valid combinations in every domain.

The multiplexing law dictates which combinations of active bits are logically sound.
While the carrier can represent 256 states, the semantic domain may restrict this to a much smaller subset of admitted combinations.
