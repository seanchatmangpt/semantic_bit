# COG8

The Semantic8 Spine maps relations to cognition. In a deterministic system, "cognition" is not artificial intelligence or emergent behavior; it is the exhaustive, bounded evaluation of constraints before action is permitted.

We call this **COG8**.

The COG8 field is the cognitive boundary of the operation. It represents the internal checklist of the machine, verifying that all preconditions, dependencies, and authorities are satisfied before execution. If `Relation64` defines the graph of dependencies, `COG8` is the evaluation of that graph for the selected operation.
