# Need9

When multiplexing semantic meanings onto an 8-bit carrier, we defined eight primary states that correspond to the eight active bits. However, the system fundamentally involves nine potential semantic positions. This section introduces Need9, the overarching necessity to accommodate and evaluate a "ninth meaning."

Need9 arises when an 8-bit boundary limits full semantic expression, demanding structural expansion beyond the immediate multiplexed byte. This part explores how to deal with this limitation through evidence, splitting, and movement.
