# RECEIPTED

The **RECEIPTED** status is the assertion of proven execution. The field has traversed an operation, and the result has been immutably recorded.

A `RECEIPTED` field is no longer just data; it is evidence. It carries the cryptographic or structural proof that a specific action occurred at a specific time, yielding a specific result.

This status is critical for audit, accountability, and the construction of trusted systems across boundaries.
