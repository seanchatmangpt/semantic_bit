# Status8 Receipt

A **Status8 Receipt** is the immutable record that a field's status was evaluated and acted upon.

It binds the original state of the field, the `Status8` value discovered, the selection rule applied, and the outcome of that decision into a single verifiable artifact.

By generating a Status8 Receipt, the system proves that it obeyed the rules of semantic multiplexing. It leaves a deterministic trail showing exactly why a field was admitted, warned, blocked, or rejected.
