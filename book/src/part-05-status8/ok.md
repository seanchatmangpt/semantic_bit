# OK

The **OK** status is the assertion of absolute validity. It is the highest assurance a field can carry.

When a field holds the `OK` status, it guarantees:
- The structural boundary of the field is intact.
- The meaning contained within the field adheres to all selection rules.
- The field is immediately ready for subsequent operations.

An `OK` field requires no further resolution. It is the green light of the deterministic engine.
