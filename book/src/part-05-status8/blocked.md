# BLOCKED

The **BLOCKED** status is an assertion of external failure. The field itself may be structurally sound, but it cannot proceed.

A field becomes `BLOCKED` when a dependency—another field, a resource, or an external system—refuses to provide the necessary conditions for progression. 

A `BLOCKED` field is not invalid; it is merely waiting. It requires intervention or a change in environmental state before it can transition to an actionable status.
