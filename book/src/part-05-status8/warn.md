# WARN

The **WARN** status is the assertion of conditional validity. The field has passed structural boundaries, but its contents carry an anomaly.

A `WARN` status indicates that a rule was triggered, but the rule did not demand failure. The system chose to admit the field, but it attaches the warning to ensure that subsequent operations are aware of the risk.

A `WARN` field is actionable, but it may produce degraded or unexpected results downstream. It demands attention without halting the process.
