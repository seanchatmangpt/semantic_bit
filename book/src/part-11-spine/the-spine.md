# The Spine

The Semantic8 Spine is the deterministic sequence of evaluation that bounds all consequential action within the system. It is a funnel, narrowing from raw reality to recorded history.

Instead of jumping directly from a request to an action, the Spine requires that meaning passes through a strict succession of primitive fields. Each field acts as a gate, ensuring that the necessary preconditions have been satisfied before proceeding to the next stage.

The progression is absolute:
1. Status8
2. ConditionCode8
3. Operation64
4. Relation64
5. COG8
6. CONSTRUCT8
7. Receipt

There are no shortcuts. A step cannot be skipped. By enforcing this sequence, the Spine guarantees that every action is fully bounded, authorized, and evidenced before it ever occurs.
