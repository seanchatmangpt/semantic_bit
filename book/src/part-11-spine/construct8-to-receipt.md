# CONSTRUCT8 to Receipt

The conclusion of a `CONSTRUCT8` operation is not mere silence. The Semantic8 Spine demands that every consequence must leave an immutable trace. This is the **Receipt**.

As the final step of the construction, a receipt is emitted. This receipt captures the entire path through the Spine: the initial `Status8`, the selected `ConditionCode8`, the intended `Operation64`, the resolved `Relation64`, the closed `COG8`, and the finalized `CONSTRUCT8` state.

The receipt is the cryptographic or deterministic proof that the consequence was generated lawfully, adhering to every boundary of the system.
