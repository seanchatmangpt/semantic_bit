# Receipt to Replay

The true value of the Semantic8 Spine lies not just in the original execution, but in its absolute reproducibility. This is the power of **Replay**.

Because the Spine uses bounded, 8-bit semantic primitives and emits comprehensive receipts, any historical event can be perfectly replayed. 

By feeding the original field states back into the system, the identical selection rules will fire, the identical relations will be evaluated, and the identical cognitive closure will be reached. The system becomes completely deterministic, allowing for audit, debugging, and recovery without the risk of divergent outcomes.
