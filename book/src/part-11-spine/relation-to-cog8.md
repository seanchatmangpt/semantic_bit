# Relation to COG8

As relations are resolved and evidence is gathered, the system builds toward a point of cognitive closure. This state is represented by **COG8**.

`COG8` (Cognitive Field) is the sum of all preceding evaluations. Once all `Relation64` constraints are met—once the action is `EVIDENCED`, `AUTHORIZED`, `FRESH`, and `CONSISTENT`—the cognitive field is declared `CLOSED`.

A closed `COG8` field is the definitive signal that the system has finished "thinking". All required knowledge is present and bounded. No further external inquiry is needed, and no ambiguity remains. The system is now locally actionable.
