# COG8 to CONSTRUCT8

`CONSTRUCT8` is the primitive of bounded manufacture. It takes the closed intent and the established authority, and oversees the physical (or digital) generation of the result. 

Because `CONSTRUCT8` is preceded by `COG8`, it never guesses. It never acts on partial information. It simply executes the fully authorized, unambiguous mandate, ensuring that the constructed artifact (the state change, the message, the file) perfectly aligns with the evaluated boundaries.
