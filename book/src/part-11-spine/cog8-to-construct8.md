# COG8 to CONSTRUCT8

The transition from cognitive state to bounded construction is the final gate in the decision core.

In the Semantic8 Spine, `COG8` represents the cognitive status of a fact—whether it is evidenced, authorized, and fresh. `CONSTRUCT8` represents the transition of that fact into an admitted, typed, and bounded construction.

$$
\boxed{\text{COG8} \xrightarrow{\text{admit}} \text{CONSTRUCT8}}
$$

## 11.6.1 The Construction Gate

A construction is not merely a data structure. It is a semantic event. A system "constructs" a consequence only after the cognitive field has selected for admission.

If the COG8 field indicates that a fact is `AUTHORIZED` and `FRESH`, but not `EVIDENCED`, the system may refuse to admit the construction.

The law of construction admission:

$$
\boxed{\textbf{A construction is only admitted into the system if the cognitive field carries the required evidence and authority.}}
$$

## 11.6.2 The Eight CONSTRUCT8 Meanings

The `CONSTRUCT8` field bounds the lifecycle of the construction itself:

| Position | Meaning | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | `DECLARED` | The construction has been named. |
| 2 | `TYPED` | The construction has an admitted type. |
| 3 | `AUTHORIZED` | The construction is authorized by rule. |
| 4 | `EVIDENCED` | The construction is backed by evidence. |
| 5 | `BOUNDED` | The construction has reached its semantic boundary. |
| 6 | `ADMITTED` | The construction is admitted to the next layer. |
| 7 | `RECEIPTED` | A receipt has been generated for this construction. |
| 8 | `REPLAYABLE` | The construction can be re-validated from its receipt. |

## 11.6.3 From Thought to Thing

The COG8 field is "internal" to the observer. The CONSTRUCT8 field is the "external" manifestation of that observation.

When a person presents a badge:
1. COG8 determines if the badge is `AUTHORIZED` and the time is `FRESH`.
2. CONSTRUCT8 admits a `GRANTED` record as a `TYPED` and `BOUNDED` construction.

This transition ensures that the system never acts on "loose" cognition. Every action must be captured in a bounded construction.
